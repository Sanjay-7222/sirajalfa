Hey, I'm Siraj

A wedding photographer based in Iyer Bungalow, with a passion for capturing weddings across the Madurai and beyond. I truly love what I do, and I consider it an incredible honor to Photograph such special moments in your life.

My photography style seamlessly blends editorial, documentary, and nostalgic elements, allowing me to capture the raw emotions and beautiful connections that define your wedding day. While I incorporate some guided posing, I prefer to keep this to a minimum. Instead, I focus on movement, flow, and natural light to create images that evoke genuine feelings and emotions.

Most importantly, my priority is to get to know you and your unique vibe. I strive to ensure you feel relaxed, comfortable, and at ease in front of the camera, so we can capture the essence of your special day authentically.


﻿

A little about my Services

I offer destination wedding photography, Best Candid Photography for Pre-wedding and Post-wedding Outdoor shoot at Awesome Locations, Engagement, Wedding and Reception Photography in Various Traditional out-looks, Baby shower and Baby photoshoot, Birthday parties, House Warming Ceremony and much more,ensuring that no matter where you say 'I do' your moments are documented with beautiful connection & creative style.

I usually capture my life on one of the many film cameras I have in my collection. I just love the extremely nostalgic, grainy and unpredictable nature of film and how it gives a raw and real feel and this is the exact reason I offer this to all my couples looking for that nostalgic touch.

I would love to meet you both and hear all about your wedding day plans
